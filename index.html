Start Your Adventure

What is D&D?

Dungeons & Dragons is a cooperative storytelling game. You create a character, team up with friends, and go on adventures together — battling monsters, solving puzzles, and making choices that shape the story.

🎲

Roll Dice

A 20-sided die (d20) decides if your actions succeed or fail. High roll = good. Low roll = trouble!

📖

Tell a Story

One player is the Dungeon Master. They describe the world. Everyone else decides what to do next.

⚔️

Fight & Survive

When a fight breaks out, players take turns attacking. Teamwork is the key to winning.

Choose Your Class

Your class is your character's job. Each one plays differently.

⚔️

Fighter

The toughest warrior. Great for beginners. Stands in front and takes hits for the team.

Best for Beginners
🧙

Wizard

Powerful magic user. Casts spells like Fireball. Fragile but devastating from a distance.

High Damage
✨

Cleric

The healer of the group. Keeps teammates alive. Also deals damage with holy power.

Team Healer
🗡️

Rogue

Sneaky and fast. Deals massive damage from the shadows. Expert at traps and locks.

Stealth Expert
🏹

Ranger

Master archer and wilderness tracker. Great at long range and has an animal companion.

Ranged Fighter
💢

Barbarian

Goes into a RAGE for bonus damage. Has the most HP of any class. Very fun for beginners!

Best for Beginners
